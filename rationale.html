Concept

Riolabz builds things, so the site shows something being built. The hero assembles a page as you arrive, and the estimator lets a visitor plan a project before filling in any form.

Colour

Deep navy for trust, cobalt for actions, mint and yellow as small accents. A dark theme is included and follows the visitor’s system setting.

Typography

Bricolage Grotesque gives headings some character. Instrument Sans keeps body text calm and readable. Body text is 17px with lines kept under 65 characters.

Navigation

The menu keeps the structure of the current site, grouped under About, Services and Solutions, so existing visitors are not lost.

Key UX decisions

A project estimator on the homepage, a portfolio filter, case studies with a clear next step, and a contact form that is prefilled from the estimator.

Responsive

Layouts change at 1100px, 860px and 560px. The full menu becomes a single list on small screens and buttons keep comfortable tap sizes.

Accessibility

Visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, semantic landmarks, sufficient contrast in both themes, and reduced motion respected.
